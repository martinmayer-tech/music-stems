# Story Map - 0000012-web-front-end

> Each row is one step under an activity. The Requirement cell names a requirement or a gap.

## Backbone

| Activity | Step | Story | Requirement |
|----------|------|-------|-------------|
| Open the service | Load the page from the root address | US-001 | REQ-001 |
| Queue a job | Choose a file and the full stems option, then send it | US-002 | REQ-002 |
| Follow a job | See each job and its status update | US-003 | REQ-003 |
| Collect stems | Play and download each stem of a finished job | US-004 | REQ-004 |

## Gaps

- Pick a model: gap: out of scope. Step 7 adds it.
- Cancel or delete a job: gap: out of scope. The brief asks for neither.
- Log in: gap: out of scope. The chart sets no login.
